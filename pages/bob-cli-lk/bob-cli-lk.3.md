## Description

reply-reopen: a recent-activity search and fold of closed enrolled threads. A new counted reply or bounce saves the message, then reopens the chosen linker (in place, or restored from done/). It lifts any awaiting hold, resurfaces the task, records a link to the reply card, and appends the turn marker. Adds config, reports, JSON, docs, and the glossary fix.
