## Description

On desktop Obsidian, every album note and record note opens on its card in the title slot above native Properties, and that card is the note's only visible title. The album card paints its title from local data at once, hides its redundant H1 at render time, and folds its schema to one line. The record card carries the RECORD kicker, a sheet tile, and paper material, with its walk rail docked at its bottom. The bank Setup card gains the BANK kicker and tile. The Record Card and album card editor headers show the species tile and word. Every failure restores native rendering.
